Is AniPulse free? +
Yes! AniPulse is completely free to use with no premium tiers or hidden costs. All features are available to everyone.
How is my data synced? +
AniPulse pulls real-time data from the AniList GraphQL API. Your watchlist and progress are stored securely in our database.
Can I track multiple anime at once? +
Absolutely! Add as many anime as you want to your watchlist with different statuses — Watching, Completed, Planned, On Hold, or Dropped.
How do episode notifications work? +
Enable notifications in your settings and we'll send you alerts when new episodes of your tracked anime air. You can also get a weekly digest.
